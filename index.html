About me

I'm a developer and a Business Computing student at Esprit School of Business. In class and outside it, I keep coming back to the same question: how do you build software that actually helps a business run better. That's the thread running through everything I work on.

In 2026 I interned at HAICA, where I built a full-stack app from scratch — JWT auth, role-based access control, a relational database, and a Vue 3 frontend. The year before, at Banque de Tunisie et des Émirats (BTE), I did the opposite kind of work: modernizing an internal credit-management tool with Java Swing and Apache POI.

Half business, half builder. I like figuring out how an organization works, then making the software that runs it better. When I'm not coding I'm usually reading about generative AI, signing up for the next certification, or arguing about football with friends in Tunis.
